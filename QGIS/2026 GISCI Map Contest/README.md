<h1>Greater Toronto Area’s Evolving Linguistic Landscape</h1>

English and French are the official languages of Canada, but many people in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), the most populous metropolitan area in Canada and one of the most diverse in the world, speak neither at home. The map series focuses on the linguistic  evolution of census subdivisions of the Greater Toronto Area, which comprises the city of Toronto proper and its surrounding municipalities. Specifically, 3 of the maps will be choropleth maps showing  each census subdivision’s (i.e., city and town) most-spoken non-official language at home in 1981, 2001, and 2021, based on Census data from Statistics Canada. Beside the three choropleth maps are the description of the map, as well as the broader contexts at the time that contextualizes the maps. A fourth map is a political map of the Greater Toronto Area, which will help viewers know the names of the surrounding cities and their respective regions.
